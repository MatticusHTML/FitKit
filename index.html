Movement & Method

Fitness Framework

Moderate, sustainable, built for weight loss first — with muscle tone developing naturally as you go.

🎯
The Core Strategy
Your goal is fat loss while preserving and toning muscle. The formula: a moderate calorie deficit (~300–400 kcal below maintenance), compound strength movements 3x/week, and active cardio on off days. Muscle burns more calories at rest — building it while losing fat is the smartest long game.
🏋️
Strength Focus Areas
  • Squats & lunges — biggest muscle groups, biggest calorie burn
  • Deadlifts & Romanian deadlifts — posterior chain + glutes
  • Push movements (push-ups, overhead press)
  • Pull movements (rows, lat pulldowns)
  • Core planks & dead bugs — not crunches
🫀
Cardio That Works
Skip long, slow treadmill sessions.

HIIT (2x/week): 15–20 min, high intensity.

Walking (daily): 7,000+ steps. Low-intensity fat burning without muscle breakdown.
💤
Recovery Is Non-Negotiable
Muscles grow when you rest.
  • 7–9 hours of sleep every night
  • Stretch or foam roll on rest days
  • At least 1 full rest day per week
  • 2.5–3L of water daily
🥗
Protein: The Most Important Rule
Target 100–120g of protein daily. Protein at every meal AND snack. Top sources in this kit: tofu, edamame, lentils, chickpeas, black beans, hemp seeds, pea protein.
📈
Progressive Overload
Each week, add something:
  • Add 5 lbs to your lift
  • Add 1 extra rep per set
  • Reduce rest time by 15 seconds
  • Add one more set total

🔬 Key Micronutrients for Plant-Based Women
These are the micronutrients most commonly under-consumed on a plant-based diet. Nail these and everything else falls into place.
Iron Critical
Target: 18mg/day (women need 1.8× more than men on plant-based diets). Best sources in this kit: lentils, tofu, edamame, pumpkin seeds. Always pair with vitamin C (lime, bell pepper) to boost absorption by up to 3×. Avoid coffee/tea within 1 hour of iron-rich meals.
Vitamin B12 Supplement
Target: 2.4mcg/day — impossible to get adequately from plants alone. This is the #1 supplement for any plant-based eater. Take a methylcobalamin B12 supplement daily or eat nutritional yeast regularly (it's in the popcorn recipe). Deficiency causes fatigue, brain fog, and nerve damage over time.
Vitamin D Supplement
Target: 1,000–2,000 IU/day via D3 supplement (vegan D3 from lichen exists). Vitamin D controls calcium absorption, immune defense, mood regulation, and muscle function. Most people in the US are deficient. Get bloodwork done — your doctor can give you a personalized dose.
Calcium Monitor
Target: 1,000mg/day. Best plant sources: fortified oat or almond milk (~300mg/cup), edamame (175mg/cup), tahini (130mg/2 tbsp), bok choy, broccoli. Given lactose sensitivity, fortified plant milks are your main lever here. Check labels — calcium content varies widely by brand.
Zinc Monitor
Target: 8mg/day (but 12–15mg recommended for plant-based due to lower bioavailability). Critical for immune defense, skin healing, and muscle repair. Best sources in this kit: pepitas (pumpkin seeds) are the #1 plant source, followed by hemp seeds, chickpeas, and lentils.
Omega-3 (ALA) In the Kit
Target: 1.1g ALA/day minimum, but consider an algae-based DHA/EPA supplement for full benefit. Best sources in this kit: ground flaxseed (2.3g/tbsp), chia seeds (5g/oz), hemp seeds (1g/tbsp), walnuts. ALA from plants converts to DHA/EPA at only ~5–15%, so an algae supplement fills the gap.
Iodine Monitor
Target: 150mcg/day. Iodine regulates thyroid hormones that control metabolism — a big deal for weight loss. Most plant-based eaters are deficient. Use iodized sea salt, add seaweed snacks occasionally, or take a supplement with iodine. Dairy was most people's main source; removing it means you need to replace it.
Magnesium In the Kit
Target: 310–320mg/day. Magnesium powers 300+ enzymatic reactions including muscle contraction, nerve function, and sleep regulation. Best sources: dark leafy greens, black beans, edamame, pumpkin seeds, dark chocolate (GF). Most people are mildly deficient — a magnesium glycinate supplement at bedtime also improves sleep quality.
💊 Recommended Supplement Stack
①B12 (Methylcobalamin) — 1,000mcg daily or 2,500mcg 3x/week. Non-negotiable on plant-based. This is the one supplement you cannot skip.
②Vitamin D3 (vegan) — 1,000–2,000 IU daily with your fattiest meal for best absorption. Get bloodwork first to determine your actual level.
③Algae-based DHA/EPA — 250–500mg combined DHA+EPA daily. This is where fish get their omega-3s from — skip the middleman and go straight to the source.
④Magnesium Glycinate — 200–400mg before bed. Improves sleep quality, reduces muscle cramps, supports recovery. Glycinate form is gentlest on digestion.
⑤Iodine — 150mcg daily if you are not regularly using iodized salt. Important for thyroid and metabolic function, often overlooked on plant-based diets.
⑥Creatine Monohydrate — 3–5g daily (optional but evidence-backed). Increases strength, muscle recovery, and cognitive function. Especially beneficial for plant-based athletes who get zero creatine from food.

Bonus Tips
01Eat your morning meal within 2 hours of waking to kickstart metabolism — even a smoothie counts perfectly.
02Aim for 3–5 spicy meals per week, not every meal. Capsaicin works best when it surprises your system — daily use reduces its thermogenic effect.
03Rice is your friend. Cooled cooked rice develops resistant starch that feeds gut bacteria and has a lower glycemic impact than freshly cooked.
04Crunchy food requires more chewing, which slows eating and gives your brain time to register fullness. Never apologize for preferring crispy.
05Pickles and fermented foods support the gut microbiome, directly linked to easier weight management and reduced bloating.
06Smoothies are a legitimate high-protein meal — not just a snack. Add protein powder, hemp seeds, or nut butter to make them meal-worthy.
